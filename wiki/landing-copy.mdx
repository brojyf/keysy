## Recommended copy

**Keysy = key + easy.** Lead with simplicity; show AI through specific features.

| Element | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Secret management for developers |
| Headline | Keys made easy. |
| Description | Manage API keys and secrets from development to production. |
| Prelaunch status | Coming soon |
| Prelaunch action | See how it works |

Draft copy based on the [PRD](/prd). Keep **Coming soon** for unreleased features.

## Keep it short

- Headlines: at most 6 words.
- Descriptions: one sentence, ideally 14 words or fewer.
- Buttons: 2–4 words naming the action.
- One benefit per block. Remove repeated claims and filler.
- Use **keys** for the brand and **secrets** for the product category.

## Headline alternatives

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keys made easy.** | Short; expresses the name. | Needs the category label. |
| **Secret management, made easy.** | Clearer category; simplest alternative. | More generic. |
| **Your secrets. Simply managed.** | Matches the existing design. | Weaker connection to the name. |

Prefer **Keys made easy.**: the category label provides clarity without lengthening the headline.

## Official references

| Source | Observed headline | Lesson |

| [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) | “Build in a weekend Scale to millions” | Lead with the benefit. |

Supabase's **Start your project** and Resend's **Get started** name the next step directly.

## Keep claims accurate

- **Security:** encrypted at rest, with keys stored separately, is a PRD requirement;
  verify implementation before publishing. Do not claim end-to-end encryption.
- **Versions:** resolve [PR #53](https://github.com/brojyf/keysy/pull/53)'s proposed
  folder versioning before writing detailed version or rollback copy.
- **Retrievals:** say **last retrieved**, not currently running.
- **Limits:** reminders do not rotate secrets; version history is bounded.
- **Release claims:** verify SLA, SDK availability, pricing, and AI data-handling
  promises before publishing them. Design examples alone are not evidence.
