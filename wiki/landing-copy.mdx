## Keep it clear and concise

Give readers enough context to understand the product without making them work through a long explanation. Clarity matters more than a word count.

- Headlines: a short, specific benefit that is easy to scan.
- Hero descriptions: one or two short sentences explaining the product and its main advantage.
- Feature descriptions: usually one complete sentence. Add a second only when it clarifies how the feature helps.
- Buttons: short action labels, usually 2–4 words.
- One main idea per block. Remove repetition and filler; keep useful context.
- Use **keys** for the brand and **secrets** for the product category.

## Headline alternatives

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Move secrets. Keep access right.** | Specific; names the core problem and matches the hero demo. | Narrower; the description covers other features. |
| **API key management, made easy.** | Keeps the key + easy wordplay. | "Easy" is vague; longer. |
| **Keys made easy.** | Short; expresses the name. | Vague; "keys" alone is ambiguous. |
| **Secret management, made easy.** | Clearer category. | Generic; repeats the category label. |
| **Your secrets. Simply managed.** | Matches the earlier design. | Vague; weaker connection to the name. |

Prefer **Move secrets. Keep access right.**: a specific outcome beats a vague adjective, and the category label names the product.
Avoid **key management**, which usually means cryptographic key management services (KMS).

## Keep claims accurate

- **Security:** encrypted at rest, with keys stored separately, is a PRD requirement;
  verify implementation before publishing. Do not claim end-to-end encryption.
- **Versions:** follow the [PRD](/prd)'s folder versioning rules when writing
  detailed version or rollback copy.
- **Retrievals:** say **last retrieved**, not currently running.
- **Limits:** reminders do not rotate secrets; version history is bounded.
- **Release claims:** verify SLA, SDK availability, pricing, and AI data-handling
  promises before publishing them. Design examples alone are not evidence.
