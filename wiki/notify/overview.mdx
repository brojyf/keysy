---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Notification service in the Keysy monorepo."
---

`notify/` is a Go process with no HTTP server or endpoints. It joins Kafka
consumer group `notify` on topic `keysy.email` using Aiven TLS and
SASL/SCRAM-SHA-512, with the CA from `KAFKA_CA_FILE`.

It sends each message through Resend `POST /emails`. The body contains `to` and
`template` (`id` or alias, `variables`). Templates in Resend hold `from` and
`subject` defaults. The `Idempotency-Key` is the record's `id` header
(`event_id`). Every retry rebuilds the same body.

The service retries network errors, 5xx, 429 `rate_limit_exceeded`, and 409
`concurrent_idempotent_requests` up to three times with 1s, 2s, and 4s delays.
It drops other failures, messages older than 24 hours, and malformed messages.
Each drop logs "notify email dropped" with event_id, template, reason, and
offset, never the address, then commits the offset. There is no dead-letter
topic. `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` is optional and enables logging only.

Keysy has no in-app notifications: no notification center, inbox, or unread
state.
