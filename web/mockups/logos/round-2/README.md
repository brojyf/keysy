| ID | Approach | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 05 Fold | Split geometric K with DM Sans Medium | Compact silhouette; separate symbol; clear name | Initial-based marks are a familiar category |
| 06 Seal | Rounded seal with two triangular counters; Manrope SemiBold | Solid, contained silhouette; works as a small mark | Meaning needs association with the name |
| 07 Space | Opposing brackets; Space Grotesk Medium | Quiet developer-tool character; open center | Brackets are common in developer branding |
| 08 Cast | Manrope Bold uppercase; E replaced by three bars | Strong typographic rhythm; no separate ornament | E can read as a stylized Greek xi |
| 09 Editorial | Instrument Serif Regular with adjusted tracking | Editorial character; light visual weight | Fine strokes weaken at small sizes; no dedicated symbol |
| 10 Curve | Custom lowercase monoline paths | Friendly open letters; rounded terminals | More casual; less compact than the other wordmarks |

05 and 09 are the preferred directions for further refinement: 05 favors a
compact software identity; 09 gives the wordmark a more editorial personality.
These are design judgments and visual explorations, not a finalized identity.

## Research applied

- [Pentagram / Intrinsic](https://www.pentagram.com/work/intrinsic): its wordmark
  combines a structured base with characteristic curves, and the symbol,
  wordmark and type system share a consistent visual language. Applied here by
  pairing comparable stroke weights and keeping each direction internally coherent.
- [Pentagram / Satalia](https://www.pentagram.com/work/satalia): a single concept
  informs both its reduced wordmark and typographically matched lambda symbol.
  Applied here by giving each exploration one visual idea rather than combining
  several motifs. No Satalia or Intrinsic artwork was reused.
- [IBM / 8-bar](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/): the official
  guide treats optical alignment and positive/reverse rendering deliberately.
  Applied through light/dark comparisons, a larger optical size for the serif
  direction, and 28px-high placement previews. IBM-specific numeric rules were
  not transplanted to Keysy.

## Verification

Inspect all six lockups on both backgrounds and at 28px. The three independent
symbols are exported separately as `05-symbol.svg`–`07-symbol.svg`.
The combined HTML embeds all artwork, starts in monochrome, and switches the
same SVGs across the existing light/dark palettes. Visual inspection does not
establish recognition in a user study or trademark availability.
