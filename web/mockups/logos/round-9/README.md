# Keysy · authorization and credential lifecycle

[Interactive review](../../review.html#logo-33) · [Editable Figma board](https://www.figma.com/design/3QD7tbVmZnKZoLEUOD0yRl?node-id=24-2)

Options 33–36 retain all 23 earlier logos and original SVGs. All four continue using the Manrope Medium wordmark so the symbols can be compared directly.

| Number | Functional meaning | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 33 Fit | Complementary teeth face each other across negative space, expressing identity/permission matching | Complete silhouette, strong negative space, readable at 16px | May suggest an industrial connector |
| 34 Custody | Two supporting arms enclose a square secret | Simple geometry suited to centralized custody | Abstract without product context |
| 35 Rotate | Two arcs surround a core; gaps suggest replacement and renewal | Directional, suited to lifecycle concepts | May read as sync or refresh |
| 36 Namespace | Outer scope, inner path, and a secret form layers | Expresses scoped organization and isolation | Denser than other options at 16px |

The original round compared **16 Cocoa / Ice × 33 Fit** with **18 Aubergine / Celadon × 34 Custody**: a strong silhouette versus custody semantics. Palette 18 has since been removed. The simpler final approach remains one symbol and one light/dark color system.

## References and boundaries

- [OpenBao overview](https://openbao.org/docs/what-is-openbao/) calibrates the secrets-manager category. Rotation and namespaces are conceptual directions, not claims that Keysy implements those capabilities.
- [Hiro / PORTO ROCHA](https://www.portorocha.com/hiro) informs developer-readable visual language.
- [Crux / Order](https://order.design/project/crux) informs one primary concept per symbol.
- See [palette research](../../palette-research.md) for credited designers, original work, and trade-offs. These symbols were drawn by an agent in Figma, not by the referenced designers; no reference brand logo was copied.

## Files and verification

- `33.svg`–`36.svg` are combined wordmarks; `*-symbol.svg` files are standalone symbols.
- SVGs use outlined paths and `currentColor`, without external fonts or images. Figma wordmarks keep editable text.
- Figma checks covered black/white, reverse white, 28px wordmarks, and 16/24/32px standalone symbols.
- Automated checks cover all 10 current palettes, stable numbers, text/button contrast, and exact agreement between embedded and exported SVGs.
- Recognition judgments come from visual inspection, not user recognition experiments.
