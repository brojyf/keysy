# Keysy / selected design references

Applied in [new studies 15–18](round-4/README.md).

Reviewed on 2026-10-15 after the user requested simplicity, good taste, and
removal of 13/14. The confirmed category is secrets management, similar to OpenBao.

## 1. Order / Crux

- Studio: https://order.design/
- Specific case: https://order.design/project/crux
- Source observation: the case derives its symbol from the waterwheel, connecting
  a reduced graphic to the client's clean-energy business. Clear typography and
  a core black/white language support that symbol.
- Keysy interpretation: choose one secret-management idea, such as concealment
  or protected storage. Build a recognizable silhouette around it. A diagram of
  keys, entry points and storage compartments combines too many ideas.
- Take forward: one compact symbol, clear wordmark, deliberate space between them.

## 2. PORTO ROCHA / Hiro

- Studio: https://www.portorocha.com/
- Specific case: https://www.portorocha.com/hiro
- Source observation: Hiro's `/−/` mark and mono wordmark draw from the command
  line. The studio explicitly connects this construction to the developer tools.
  The case was also opened in Safari to inspect its visual presentation.
- Keysy interpretation: a concealed value or a simple container can communicate
  the product through familiar developer notation. Candidate 12 already has a
  useful starting point in its masking dots and boundaries.
- Take forward: precise strokes, few elements and a symbol that remains clear
  at navigation size. Hiro's actual slash mark, lettering and orange palette are
  not Keysy artwork and should not be transplanted.

## 3. Logosystem / Minimal

- Library: https://logosystem.co/
- Relevant collection: https://logosystem.co/logos/minimal
- Source observation: the collection can be narrowed by minimal style, symbols,
  wordmarks, negative space and monochrome. It links entries to their designers.
- Keysy interpretation: use the collection to compare silhouette economy and
  optical balance, then follow original designer case studies for the reasoning.
- Take forward: compare black/white first and judge the visible mark at 16/24/32px.
  The collection is an inspiration index, not the source of a production symbol.

## Design direction extracted for Keysy

Retain 11/12 as references, with 12 the more promising technical direction.
Favor a single concealed-value motif over combining a key with a storage diagram.
Keep the wordmark legible, of moderate weight, and optically balanced with the
symbol. Let white space and consistent geometry carry the design.

Tradeoff: a simple motif is less explanatory on its own. The name and product
context supply meaning; the mark does not need to illustrate every capability.
This is an aesthetic direction for the next drawing pass, not a claim that the
remaining candidates are finalized or independently validated.
