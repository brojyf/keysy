## Before commercial adoption

1. Search the name, standalone symbol, and combined lockup separately in the US and China, including similar names and relevant goods/services. Have the final scope and results reviewed by a trademark professional familiar with both markets.
2. For the US, include registered and pending marks plus earlier commercial use; see the [USPTO clearance-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks). The [USPTO confusion framework](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) considers both mark similarity and related goods/services.
3. For China, use word and figurative searches. [CNIPA's guidance](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/jact/front/mailpubdetail.do?sysid=13&transactId=339444) directs abstract-logo searches through figurative classification codes in the China Trademark Office search system.
4. Use [WIPO's Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index) image-similarity search as an additional check, not a substitute for national registers. Preserve the query date, territory, candidate artwork, and relevant records with the final assessment.
